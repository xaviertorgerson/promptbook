# Critical User Journeys — Invoicing

## CUJ-1 · An owner downloads last month's invoice
- **Actor:** an account owner
- **Personas:** PER-1
- **User-facing:** yes
- **Goal:** a PDF for the accountant
- **Trigger:** the owner opens Billing → Invoices.
- **Success:** the PDF downloads and its total matches the row.
- **Unwanted:** another tenant's invoice is listed.
