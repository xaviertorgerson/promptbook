# Decisions (ADRs)

## ADR-0031: Invoice lists are keyset-paginated
**Status:** Superseded
**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Decision:** Lists page by (issued_at, id).
**Why:** Offset pagination degrades on large accounts.
**Consequences:** No page numbers in the UI.
