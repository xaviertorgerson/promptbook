# Acme Billing

## Problem
Usage-based SaaS teams bill by spreadsheet; every month-end is a day of reconciliation.

## Users
Account owners who read invoices (PER-1) and finance staff who reconcile them (PER-2).

## Outcomes
- Month-end close in under an hour.
- Zero double-billed periods.

## Non-goals
Tax calculation; payment collection.
