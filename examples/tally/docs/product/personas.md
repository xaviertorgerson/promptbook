## PER-1 · Organiser
- **Role:** logs the household's shared bills and closes each month
- **Goals:**
  - when a bill arrives, log it in under a minute, so nobody has to remember it later [evidence: docs/research/interviews.md]
  - when the month ends, settle without chasing anyone, so the group chat stays friendly [evidence: docs/research/interviews.md]
- **Context:** phone first, a few times a month, no accounting knowledge [assumption: confirm in the five-household pilot]
- **Pains:**
  - rebuilding a spreadsheet every month [evidence: docs/research/interviews.md]
- **Must never:** lose a bill they logged, or show it to another household [assumption: confirm in the pilot]
- **Changes:** the save-path performance NFR and the household isolation decision
- **Evidence:**
  - docs/research/interviews.md

## PER-2 · Housemate
- **Role:** pays their share and marks it paid
- **Goals:**
  - when the organiser shares a link, see what I owe and mark it paid, without an account [evidence: docs/research/interviews.md]
- **Context:** opens a link from a group chat on a phone; may use a screen reader [assumption: ask the pilot households]
- **Must never:** need an account to settle, or see another household's bills [assumption: test in the pilot]
- **Changes:** link-based access with no account, the accessibility NFR, and the browser matrix (NFR-4)
- **Evidence:**
  - docs/research/interviews.md
